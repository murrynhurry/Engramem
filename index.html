    Sync

    Keep your notes the same on your phone and computer. Engramem saves them to a private gist on your GitHub account that only you can see.

    1. Open github.com/settings/tokens/new. The gist box is already ticked.
    2. Set Expiration to No expiration, tap Generate token, and copy it.
    3. Paste it here and tap Connect. Do the same on your other devices.

    Saves to Inbox · Enter save · Shift+Enter new line · Ctrl+Enter save and open
